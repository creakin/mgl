Do as much damage as you can to the refinery and post your highscore in the comments!

This  game was made for the Fxck Capitalism jam 2026 and is inspired by the book "How to Blow up a Pipeline" by Andreas Malm.  

In the book, Malm argues for a  radical flank in the climate movement and that violence against property might be necessary and justified in the face of the climate crisis.  Since world leaders have so far not done what is needed to preserve a liveable world we may have to take it upon ourselves to start dismantling the fossil industry. 

The game is designed to be played with keyboard(WASD) and mouse.

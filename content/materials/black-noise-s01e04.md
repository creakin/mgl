This unsteady and erratic field recording is an audio transmission from the First Tyrannic War. Hive Fleet Behemoth launched its full-scale invasion with thousands upon thousands of Mycetic Spores, followed by genetically enhanced Carnifexes, chameleonic Lictors and claw-fanged Genestealers.  
STEP INTO THE LIGHT OF THE GOD-EMPEROR transmits the planetary assault as the Ultramarines mount a counteroffensive against the Swarmlord across the cold, snow-covered landscapes of Macragge.
